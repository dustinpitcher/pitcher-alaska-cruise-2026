Shore Excursion Options

Pick one per port day. Family favorites are tagged. Prices are estimated per person through Princess.

🎢 Icy Strait Point — June 30

ZipRider — World's Largest Zipline Top Pick Kids Love It

5,330 feet, 1,300 ft drop, 60 mph top speed. Gondola ride through rainforest. Min height 48" — both kids qualify!

⏱ 2 hours | ~$99-129/person

Alaska Salmon Hatchery Family

See thousands of salmon, learn about conservation. Pairs perfectly with the ZipRider.

⏱ 1.5 hours | ~$45-65/person

Tide Pool & Rainforest Walk Value

Guided nature walk through tidal pools and old-growth forest. Great wildlife spotting.

⏱ 2 hours | ~$55-75/person

ATV Rainforest Adventure Adventure

Side-by-side ATV tour through Tongass Rainforest. Thrilling and fun for the whole family.

⏱ 3 hours | ~$189-229/person

Self-Guided Adventure Center Free!

Climb tower, ropes course, rainforest trail. At your own pace. Included in port fee.

⏱ 2-3 hours | Free

💡 Recommended combo: ZipRider + Salmon Hatchery (~3.5 hours, ~$160/person)

🐋 Juneau — July 1

Whale Watching + Mendenhall Glacier Top Pick

3-hour boat whale watching + 2 hours at the glacier. Two iconic Alaska experiences in one tour. Can't miss this one.

⏱ 5-6 hours | ~$179-229/person

Mendenhall Glacier & Dog Sledding Kids Love It

Meet actual sled dogs, learn about mushing, walk the glacier. The dogs are the real highlight.

⏱ 3-4 hours | ~$149-189/person

Mt. Roberts Tramway + Glacier Easy Day

Scenic tramway ride to city overlook, then shuttle to Mendenhall Glacier. Easy accessibility.

⏱ 3-4 hours | ~$89-129/person

Penguin Colony + Whitewater Rafting Adventure

Visit aquarium to see Adelie penguins, then raft the Chilkat River. Unique combo!

⏱ 5-6 hours | ~$189-229/person

💡 Recommended: Whale Watching + Mendenhall Glacier (the classic Alaska combo)

🏛️ Ketchikan — July 2

Rainforest Walk + Totem Bight Top Pick Culture

Guided Tongass rainforest trail, then visit historic totem poles. Authentic Native culture experience.

⏱ 2-3 hours | ~$59-89/person

Salmon Fishing + Hatchery Hands-On

Cast a line for salmon, then visit the creek hatchery. Great family activity.

⏱ 3-4 hours | ~$129-159/person

Saxman Native Village + Creek Street Culture

Guided tour of historic totem village, then explore the famous boardwalk.

⏱ 2-3 hours | ~$69-99/person

Bear Viewing Cruise Wildlife

Boat tour to view coastal brown bears in their natural habitat. Spectacular scenery.

⏱ 3-4 hours | ~$149-189/person

Heron Island Cruise + Salmon Bake All-Inclusive

Scenic cruise to Heron Island, salmon bake lunch at lodge. Classic Alaskan experience.

⏱ 5-6 hours | ~$149-179/person

💡 Recommended: Rainforest + Totem Bight (best value and cultural experience)

⚠️ Critical Reminders

  • BUG SPRAY — Denali mosquitoes in June are serious. Treat pants with permethrin before leaving if possible.
  • Break in hiking boots — don't wear new boots for the first time in Denali.
  • NO cotton for base layers. Merino wool or synthetic only. Cotton stays wet and gets cold.
  • Use packing cubes — cabin B525 is a Mini-Suite (decent space) but not huge.
  • Label all bags with names and contact info.
